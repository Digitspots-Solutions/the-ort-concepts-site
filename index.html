Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Pension Fund Administrator (PFA)?

A PFA is a licensed entity that manages Retirement Savings Accounts (RSAs) on behalf of pension contributors. Winning Woman Retirement Services Limited is registered with PENCOM to provide these services.

How do I open an RSA with Winning Woman?

Simply contact us via phone or WhatsApp at +234 802 860 9132. We'll guide you through the simple registration process, which requires basic identification and employment details.

Can I switch from my current PFA to Winning Woman?

Yes! You can transfer your RSA to Winning Woman at any time. The process is straightforward, and we'll handle all documentation with your current administrator.

How are my pension contributions invested?

Your funds are invested in a diversified portfolio of PENCOM-approved instruments including government securities, corporate bonds, and money market instruments, managed by experienced asset management professionals.

When can I access my pension benefits?

You can access your retirement benefits upon reaching 50 years of age (or the retirement age specified by your employer), upon voluntary retirement, or in cases of temporary or permanent disability.

How often will I receive account statements?

You receive quarterly statements showing your account balance, contributions received, investment returns, and other relevant information.

What happens to my pension if I change jobs?

Your RSA moves with you throughout your career. Simply notify your new employer of your existing RSA details, and they'll continue making contributions to the same account.

Are there charges for pension administration?

Yes, administrative and investment management fees are charged as approved by PENCOM. These are clearly disclosed in your account statements and are competitive with industry standards.
